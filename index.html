Your Torn key

HBountyTracker needs its own Public Only key. Make one in Torn's API key settings, pick Public Only, and name it HBountyTracker so you can spot it in your key log.

  • Your FFScouter key is optional. It goes in its own field and adds fair fight estimates.
  • Don't use your FFScouter key as the Torn key. It's a Custom key that can't read the bounty board, so it won't work.
  • Both keys stay in this browser and only ever go to Torn and FFScouter. The tool is open source, so you can read every line that touches them on GitHub.

Find targets

Rewards take 250k or 1.5m, and count per hit: one attack claims one bounty, the biggest on that target. Fair Fight Score is the fight you want: Easy fights puts the targets closest to it first, and High value shows the biggest bounties within 1.5 of it. It also caps the board, which you can change there.

Pace and live checks

Torn allows 100 a minute across every tool and script you run. Leave room for the rest.

Okay targets at the top of High value and Easy fights get re-checked every 90 seconds. Other Okay targets get re-checked every 10 to 30 minutes, slower the longer they have been offline. Re-checks never use more than 25% of your pace. Hospital timers are never re-checked: the target gets one check when the timer ends, even in a background tab. Other re-checks pause while this tab is in the background. After 30 minutes with no click, key press, scroll or touch, every call stops until you resume.

Sound plays for the same moments. The Sound button at the top turns all sound off.
